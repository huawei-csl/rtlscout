16-lane posit dot-product-accumulate unit `pdpu_top`.

Computes  result = acc + Va . Vb  =  acc + a0*b0 + a1*b1 + ... + a15*b15
as one fused operation:
- inputs: two vectors of N=16 posit(8,2) elements, packed little-endian into
  operands_a[127:0] / operands_b[127:0] (element i at bits [8i+7:8i]), and a
  posit(16,2) accumulator acc[15:0];
- output: posit(16,2) result_o[15:0] (mixed-precision accumulate).

Parameters: N=16, posit(8,2) inputs, posit(16,2) accumulator and result,
ALIGN_WIDTH=14, CARRY_W=5, SUM_W=19.

Datapath (combinational): posit decode of all 33 operands; exact 4x4-bit
mantissa products; signed regime/exponent sums; maximum-exponent search;
mantissa alignment into a fixed ALIGN_WIDTH=14 window (bits shifted beyond
the window are DISCARDED - the unit is deliberately inexact vs ideal posit
arithmetic); two's-complement carry-save accumulation mod 2^20; leading-zero
normalization; posit encode with round-to-nearest-even on the packed
regime/exponent/fraction field.

REQUIRED behavior is BIT-EXACTNESS to the reference implementation for all
inputs, including its architectural truncation, its modular-arithmetic corner
cases, and its handling of NaR (0x80 / 0x8000 decode to zero contributions -
the reference does not implement special NaR propagation). The testbench's
expected outputs are generated by simulating the reference RTL itself.

A correct starting implementation is provided in starting_point.py -
start from it and optimize. Any transformation is legal as long as result_o
remains bit-identical for every input.
